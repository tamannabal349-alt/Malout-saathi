# Malout Saathi - Your Climate Saathi for Farming

Problem: Malout ke kisanon ko mausam ki jankari aur mandi ke sahi daam ka pata Punjabi me nahi milta.

Solution:
- Weather Alert: Baarish/Toofan se pehle awaz me chetavni
- Mandi Bhav: Malout, Bathinda, Abohar ka daily rate
- Fasal Salah: Paani bachao aur sustainable kheti ki salah Punjabi voice me
- Pest Detection: Fasal ki photo se bimari pehchano

Impact: Climate change se ladne me madad, paani ki bachat aur kisan ki kamai badhana.
